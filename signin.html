Sign in or Get started

You create your ZaroPay account and sign in inside the ZaroPay mobile app, using your phone number and a one-time verification code (OTP) sent by SMS.

Get startedSign in

The ZaroPay app is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

How it works

  1. Get started or sign in. Open the ZaroPay app and tap Get started (new users) or Sign in (existing users).
  2. Enter your phone number. Choose your country and type your mobile number.
  3. Receive a one-time code (OTP). We send a 6-digit verification code to that number by SMS.
  4. Enter the code. Type the code in the app to confirm the number is yours. You are signed in.

When we send you a verification code (OTP)

  • Get started: when you create a new ZaroPay account.
  • Sign in: when you log in, or sign in again on a new phone.
  • Change number: when you move your account to a new phone number.

About the SMS

  • The message says: "Your ZaroPay code is 482013. It expires in 10 minutes. Do not share this code with anyone."
  • Each code works once and expires after a few minutes.
  • ZaroPay staff will never ask you for your code. If someone asks, do not share it.
  • Didn't get a code? Check the number, wait a minute and tap Resend code. Still nothing? Email info@zaropay.ng.
